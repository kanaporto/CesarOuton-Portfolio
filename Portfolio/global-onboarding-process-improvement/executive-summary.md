# Revamp Onboarding A to Z
## Executive summary

I identified that employee IT onboarding varied across regions and took the lead in structuring a global improvement effort. I designed the discovery approach, used Miro to make regional friction visible, brought stakeholders together and helped establish a shared baseline that could accommodate local requirements. The project remains active, with later implementation and governance continuing under other colleagues.

- **Global process leadership:** Recognised a cross-regional problem, analysed the current process and took ownership of the initial discovery and alignment work.
- **Designed the approach:** Coordinated regional stakeholders across APAC, AMER, India and EMEA; planned the discussions; used a Miro Board to capture and group issues; and travelled to Dublin to facilitate in-person sessions.
- **Created a shared foundation:** Helped the regions agree on an onboarding baseline. The brainstorming documented 32 reported friction points; findings and opportunities were grouped, and I requested metrics to support a formal intake. The request is documented, but the metrics and intake approval are not.
- **Follow-on improvements:** The baseline was applied and informed improvements including an onboarding application pre-installed on laptops through MDM, clearer user instructions, an improved runbook, better Recruitment-to-IT coordination for laptop-shipping information, and improvements to some day-one service access.
- **Ongoing product governance:** A global committee was put into operation, treating onboarding as a product with weekly cross-regional meetings. The project continues; I established the early foundation but did not lead its later weekly governance.
- **Scope distinction:** Laptop preparation and update checks were covered by a broader Zero Touch initiative already underway; Zero Touch did not originate from this brainstorming.
- **Evidence limits:** The project records support the discovery themes and metrics request. Later implementation and continued governance are based on my direct account. I make no quantified impact claims because I no longer have access to the employer’s internal metrics.
