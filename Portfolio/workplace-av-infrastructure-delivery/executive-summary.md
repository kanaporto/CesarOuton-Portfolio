# End-to-End Workplace AV Infrastructure Delivery
## Executive summary

- **Project:** I led the client-side AV workstream for an HQ refit supporting remote-first work, optional office attendance and global team gatherings.
- **End-to-end ownership:** As Infrastructure Project Manager, I took the AV workstream from room requirements and site constraints through planning, procurement, vendor/construction coordination, delivery, validation, operational handover and surplus-equipment decommissioning.
- **Cross-team control:** I tracked owners, actions and dependencies in Asana; ran regular vendor and stakeholder reviews; coordinated site access, purchase orders, installers, issue resolution and AV spend approvals within my remit. Additional budget needs went to the overall-project team.
- **Operational readiness:** I coordinated room-resource setup with Systems, readiness communication and handover, then arranged decommissioning of surplus equipment.
- **Reported outcome:** Career-review and promotion materials report three gathering spaces delivered on schedule, with AV reused and upgraded. Other project records describe two Zoom Rooms, a boardroom and circulation areas; the sources do not establish that these descriptions map one-to-one.
- **Technical decision:** I travelled to HQ to test camera placement in an additional room. The room height and camera angles would compromise the view, so I shared test photos, aligned stakeholders on a suitable position and coordinated a furniture redesign.
- **Delivery framework:** I use the ITIL Version 5 lifecycle retrospectively to explain the sequence—Discover → Design and Plan → Deliver and Validate → Operate and Support → Decommission and Close → Continual Improvement. This portfolio framing does not mean the company formally used ITIL for the project.
- **Evidence limits:** Reliable cost or operational metrics are not available to me, so I make no quantified savings or service-performance claims.
