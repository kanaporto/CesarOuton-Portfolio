# ORBIT — AI-assisted IT operations

## Executive summary

I led the operational definition and AI-assisted iteration of ORBIT, a solution design for coordinating operational work. The assistant investigates and proposes; company-controlled rules determine which actions are authorised; a separate verification step checks the resulting state.

The case demonstrates how I translated operational requirements into rules and authority, integration contracts, and acceptance criteria. It covers two simulated journeys—recovering on-call coverage and responding to a critical service incident—using fictional data. The package presents design decisions and evaluation criteria; it does not claim production outcomes.
