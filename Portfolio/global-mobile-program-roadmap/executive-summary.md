## Results

The time savings and delivery-time improvements below were confirmed against the prior process during program follow-up.

- **Payroll automation:** I proposed and functionally designed the stipend-report-to-Workday automation, led Payroll/Talent Systems coordination and validation, and resolved follow-up issues with the script owner. It removed manual input and duplicate-payment review, saving a confirmed **15 Payroll hours per month**.
- **MCP server:** I designed a guarded two-phase AI integration—read-only first, then read/write. The read/write phase was approved and working in the provider sandbox; production rollout remained in progress.
- **China invoices:** Transitioned manual processing to the mobility provider, confirming **70–96 hours saved annually**.
- **AI support-agent CSAT:** In **Q2 2026**, the Slack/Moveworks agent achieved **96.3% positive five-star ratings across 569 tickets** in its post-answer survey; tickets were managed in Jira.
- **Knowledge operations:** Led an AI-supported cross-functional process with the mobility provider, replacing manual spreadsheet updates with a shared source that kept agreed support-content changes aligned.
- **India procurement:** Redesigned approval and carrier-ordering steps, reducing device delivery from **4–5 weeks to 2–3 days**.
- **Vendor management:** Owned the third-party supplier relationship, annual purchase orders and contract discussions for the mobile-services program.
- **Documentation and device strategy:** Reorganised **220 knowledge articles and internal guides** and defined global device models with region-specific stock levels.
- **MDM readiness:** Enrolled corporate iPhones in Apple Business Manager across **16 carriers in 15 countries**, growing the registered fleet from several hundred to around 1,500. The subsequent MDM deployment remained with the endpoint engineering team.
- **Service access:** Restored the global mobile-support channel and coordinated an **8-hour response SLA**.

## Project at a glance

I designed and led a global employee mobile-services program, turning fragmented regional processes into a prioritised roadmap and measurable service improvements. The 2025 roadmap was completed in full; the 2026 roadmap puts AI at the centre of the next stage. I managed delivery in Jira within the global IT organisation's OKRs and reported progress and outcomes to management and senior executives.

- **Scale:** **16 carriers across 15 countries**; approximately 4,000 active participants—around 2,500 on stipend and 1,500 with a company-provided line and phone—within a potential population of 8,000+ full-time employees at a global company with 10,000+ employees.
- **Team leadership:** Supervised **seven Mobile Ambassadors**, each responsible for the operational work in their region; I set global improvement priorities and oversaw smooth program delivery.
- **Roadmap status:** 2025 completed in full. In 2026, AI/MCP integration was **in progress**, global device strategy and ABM readiness were **complete**, and further operating-model and regional improvements were **planned or not started**.

## Leadership and delivery

I designed the program, set global improvement priorities, managed the supplier relationship and commercial work, coordinated delivery, and carried completed changes into operations. I led an AI-supported cross-functional process that kept internal and provider support knowledge aligned. Where technical implementation belonged to a specialist team, I led the initiative and partnered with its implementation owner. This combination of global roadmap ownership, vendor management, AI-enabled improvement and measurable service outcomes demonstrates my readiness for **Global IT Operations, IT Program Management and Service Delivery leadership**.
